# GramResolve MVP Demo Flow

## 3-minute story

1. Citizen opens GramResolve and reports a village problem.
2. Citizen enters a clear title, description and location.
3. Submission triggers automatic AI triage.
4. The response shows category, severity, responsible department, SLA and AI summary.
5. Panchayat view shows the AI-prioritized queue and SLA/escalation signals.
6. Citizen can open a report to see the accountability timeline.
7. Official workflow continues with assignment, status changes, resolution evidence and citizen verification.

## Recommended demo grievance

**Title:** Water pipeline leaking near government school

**Description:** A major water pipeline is leaking beside the school. Dirty water is collecting on the road and families are struggling to get clean water. Children are walking through the flooded area.

**Location:** Ward 4, near Government Primary School

This wording intentionally contains enough signal for the triage engine to classify the complaint as a water-related, high-priority issue and route it toward Water & Sanitation.
